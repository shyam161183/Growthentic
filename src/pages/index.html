Questions owners ask us

What does "no upfront fees" mean?

You do not pay a large setup fee or sign a long contract before you see value. Plans start on a light monthly retainer and run month-to-month. One-time projects such as a website, Shopify build or app are quoted separately.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. All monthly plans are month-to-month with no lock-in. We ask that you agree clear payment terms, and we aim to earn every renewal.

Who pays for ad spend?

You do, directly to Google, Meta and the other platforms, from your own ad accounts. We never add a markup, and you can see every cent.

How do you use AI, and who checks the work?

AI does the heavy lifting on content, creative, reporting and campaign builds. Senior strategists review and improve everything before it goes live.

How will I know it is working?

You get a live dashboard and a plain-language monthly review that report your real cost per lead, booking or sale, not vanity metrics.

Where are you based, and does the time zone matter?

We are a fully remote team based in the EU and we work in EU time zones. Everything is delivered digitally, with WhatsApp, calls and scheduled video meetings.
