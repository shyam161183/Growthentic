Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Growthentic collects through growthentic.eu, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who we are

Growthentic is a digital marketing agency based in Kaunas, Lithuania (EU). We are the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us by phone or WhatsApp at +370 632 36893.

What data we collect

  • Details you send us. When you use a form on this site or message us, we receive what you provide: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address if you give one, business name, website, the services you are interested in, your budget or ad spend range, and your message.
  • Messages and calls. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by phone, we keep the conversation so we can follow up.
  • Usage data. Only if you accept analytics cookies: pages visited, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site.
  • Marketing Check answers. The free Marketing Check runs in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you choose to share the result with us.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare audits, proposals and quotes: steps taken at your request before a contract, and your consent when you tick the consent box.
  • To deliver services to clients and handle invoicing: performance of a contract and our legal obligations.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it: your consent to analytics cookies.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam: our legitimate interest in running the business safely.

We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similar effects on you, and we do not sell it.

Cookies and similar storage

  • Essential (always on). We store your cookie choice in your browser so the banner does not reappear on every visit.
  • Analytics (only with your consent). Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) that last up to 14 months.

You can change your choice at any time using Cookie preferences in the footer. Videos on our Work page load from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) only when you press play.

Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the business, under contracts that protect it:

  • Vercel (website hosting)
  • Formspree (receiving form submissions)
  • Google (Analytics, if you consent, and website fonts)
  • Meta (WhatsApp, if you message us there)
  • Our accounting and business software providers, for clients

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become a project: up to 24 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client and billing records: for the length of the engagement and as long as Lithuanian accounting and tax law requires afterwards.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and you can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. To make a request, call or WhatsApp +370 632 36893. We reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (vdai.lrv.lt) or the data protection authority in your own country.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, access to enquiries is limited to our team, and we use providers with strong security practices. No system is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information such as health or payment details through our forms.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
